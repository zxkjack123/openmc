--- Analysis ---
History-based: 11.5s total, 232k particles/s (primary benchmark)
Event-based: 320s total, 7.8k particles/s (~30x slower with 64 threads)
  - Event overhead dominates for small problems (50k particles)
  - Surface crossings: 60.4s (19%), Collisions: 22.0s (7%)
  - Remaining ~73% is calculate_xs (not separately reported in timer output)
  - Event-based mode is the GPU porting target (natural SIMT mapping)
k-eff agreement: Both modes produce identical k-eff = 1.16036 +/- 0.00063
  (expected range for PWR pin cell: ~1.1-1.3) ✓
